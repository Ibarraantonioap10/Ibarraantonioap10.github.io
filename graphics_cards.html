Project 1 :NV1 graphics card

The NV1 graphics card was released in 1995, it has the power to handle both 2d and 3d video the best part at it's time it was capable of allowing pc users to play games on their platform despite these innovations it struggled with incompatibility with Microsoft DirectX that limited game support.

Project 2:5070 Graphics card

The 5070 Graphics card Released on August 31, 2026 and is one of the best in the world having 6144 graphics-processing cores and Tensor Cores (supporting FP4/FP6/INT8/FP8), VRAM of 12GB seventh-generation graphics memory, Boost Clock of 2510 MHz, TDP of 250W and a Launching Price of $549.

Project 3:5090 Graphics card

The 5090 Graphics card Released on January 30, 2025 and is one of the most powerful cards to date designed for top-tier gaming, AI workloads and advanced content creation, including 32GB of seventh-generation graphics memory, CUDA Cores 21,760, TENSOR Cores 680 (5th-gen, FP4 support),Boost Clock 2010/2410 MHz, and launching price of $1,999 manufacturer's suggested retail price.
