Our Mission here at NVIDIA

Welcome to Nvidia Company page here we'll talk about the global technology leader in building the best products in the world such as processing units, GPU, computer systems, and the most known to man software for artificial intelligence followed by accelerated computing. In 1999 Nvidia ignited the modern era with gaming and Ai upgrades far ahead of its time making it what it is today as the years go by technology advances and the company has new challenges to face creating new upgrades for the people such as chips and networking hardware. The company is set not only on making chips and hardware, they are also investing time in making the best Ai models in the world making it user friendly and easy to access for anyone, especially people with disabilities so that they can be part of our world in enjoying the product by growing and learning together as a family.

The Nvidia company was founded in April 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris (one of its co-founders), and another co-founder, with great leadership to lead others. The company CEO Jensen Huang started the company and is now the CEO till this day. Due to his great ideas and plans he was able to save the company from bankruptcy into the 1900s making him expand into GPUs and other amazing products. The great CEO was an electrical engineer after earning his master's degree at Stanford University he launched Nvidia in 1993 from a Denny's restaurant in San Jose, California. Till this day the company is making the top products in the world in gaming and in Ai Module.
